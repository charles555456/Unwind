Point files in this folder are made from public-domain images.

astronaut.bin
  Bruce McCandless II on the first untethered spacewalk, STS-41B, 7 Feb 1984.
  NASA photo S84-27017. Public domain (NASA).
  https://images.nasa.gov/details/s84-27017

rocket.bin
  NASA's PACE mission lifting off on a SpaceX Falcon 9 from Space Launch
  Complex 40, Cape Canaveral, 8 Feb 2024, 1:33 a.m. EST.
  NASA photo KSC-20240208-PH-KMO01_0004 (NASA/Kevin Davis and Kevin O'Connell).
  Public domain (NASA).
  https://images.nasa.gov/details/KSC-20240208-PH-KMO01_0004

The conversion keeps only the subject (black space for the astronaut; a
levels stretch with a soft falloff for the rocket's hazy night sky) and samples
brighter areas more densely. Earlier versions also used Michelangelo's
Creation of Adam (public domain); see git history. Format: b'UPTS', u16 width, u16 height, u32 count, then
u16 x, u16 y, u8 brightness per point, little-endian.
