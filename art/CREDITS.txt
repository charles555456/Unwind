Point files in this folder are made from public-domain images.

astronaut.bin
  Bruce McCandless II on the first untethered spacewalk, STS-41B, 7 Feb 1984.
  NASA photo S84-27017. Public domain (NASA).
  https://images.nasa.gov/details/s84-27017

hands.bin
  Michelangelo, The Creation of Adam (detail), c. 1512, Sistine Chapel.
  Public domain. Source file: Wikimedia Commons,
  "Michelangelo - Creation of Adam (cropped).jpg".

saturn.bin
  Saturn seen by Voyager 2 in August 1981, NASA/JPL (PIA01364). Public domain.
  https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA01364
  Used at the centre of the private System page.

The conversion keeps only the subject (black space for the astronaut, a
colour key plus region filter for the hands) and samples brighter areas
more densely. Format: b'UPTS', u16 width, u16 height, u32 count, then
u16 x, u16 y, u8 brightness per point, little-endian.
